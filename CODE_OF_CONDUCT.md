# Code of Conduct

MedSystem1 contributors are expected to maintain a professional, respectful, and technically constructive environment.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publication of private information, and unsafe handling of patient or sensitive data are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit, reject, lock, or remove contributions or interactions that violate these expectations or create material safety, privacy, licensing, or security risk.

For conduct concerns that should not be public, use a private repository contact channel when one is published. Do not include patient information or credentials in any report.
